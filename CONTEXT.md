# Arsenal

Unofficial study tool for one student platoon at The Basic School. The Academics officer produces the material. The platoon studies it together. Arsenal is not a United States Marine Corps website or product.

## Language

**The Basic School**:
The Marine Corps course this platoon is attending.
_Avoid_: TBS website, schoolhouse site

**Platoon**:
The one student platoon Arsenal serves.
_Avoid_: class, company, cohort

**Academics**:
The platoon billet that decides what the platoon studies in Arsenal. One student holds it.
_Avoid_: instructor, faculty, school

**Admin**:
The student holding Academics. The only person who produces the platoon's material in Foundry.
_Avoid_: owner, operator, instructor

**Student**:
A member of the platoon who studies in Academy.
_Avoid_: viewer, user, trainee

**Foundry**:
The studio where the admin produces the platoon's library.
_Avoid_: backend, CMS, authoring tool

**Academy**:
The place the platoon studies the library.
_Avoid_: LMS, classroom, portal

**Library**:
The platoon's shared catalog of books, wiki entries, artifacts, and assessments.
_Avoid_: workspace, course, private notebook

**Discussion**:
A private tutor conversation started by one student.
_Avoid_: class chat, shared thread
